# Troubleshooting

Plain-language help for the common "why didn't that work?" moments.

## "It says a capability isn't available"

Availability depends on what's enabled in *this* deployment and on what you're
permitted to use. Ask "what's available to me?" to see the current picture. If
something you expected is missing, it may be disabled here, not configured, or
not granted to you — an administrator can enable or grant it.

## "A connector or tool isn't working"

- Confirm it's actually available to you (ask "what tools are available?").
- If it was just added or connected, give it a moment and try again.
- If it still fails, the external service or its setup may be the issue — an
  administrator can check the connector.

## "My job is running forever / failed"

- Longer tasks take longer — check the job status first.
- If it failed, ask "why did my job fail?" for a plain explanation and next
  step. Re-running after fixing the input often resolves it.

## "Where did my result go?"

- Background results arrive when the job finishes — check the job, then ask for
  the result again.
- Results also live with the conversation/session they were produced in; reopen
  that session to find them.

## "The assistant won't do something"

- Some actions need a capability that isn't enabled or granted here — ask what's
  available.
- Some requests are intentionally out of scope for product help (for example,
  internal engineering details) — ask directly and specifically if you need
  those.

## Still stuck?

Describe what you did, what you expected, and what happened. That's enough to
get a specific answer or a clear "here's who can enable this."
